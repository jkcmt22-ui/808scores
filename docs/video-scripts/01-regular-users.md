# Video 1: Regular Users (Parents & Fans)

**Target Length:** 3-4 minutes
**Tone:** Friendly, encouraging, community-focused
**Audience:** Parents at games, fans following from home, new users

---

## Full Script

### SCENE 1: Introduction (15 sec)

**Screen:** Hawaii Sports Center logo / app homepage

**Voiceover:**
> "Welcome to Hawaii Sports Center - Hawaii's home for high school sports. Whether you're a parent in the stands or a fan following from home, this quick guide will show you how to stay connected to every game."

---

### SCENE 2: Sign Up (45 sec)

**Screen:** Login page → Phone entry → OTP verification

**Voiceover:**
> "Getting started takes less than a minute. Tap 'Get Started' and enter your phone number. You'll receive a 6-digit verification code via text - enter it to complete sign-up."

**UI Highlights:**
- Phone number input field
- "Send Code" button
- OTP entry boxes (show auto-submit when complete)
- Terms acceptance checkbox

**Voiceover (continued):**
> "Accept the terms of service, and you're in. You can also sign up with email if you prefer."

---

### SCENE 3: Finding Games (45 sec)

**Screen:** Home page → Live games → Schedule

**Voiceover:**
> "The home screen shows you what's happening right now. Live games appear at the top with real-time scores. Scroll down to see today's schedule and recent final scores."

**UI Highlights:**
- "LIVE" badge on active games
- Score display
- Sport filter tabs
- Date navigation arrows

**Voiceover (continued):**
> "Use the sport filter to focus on what you care about - football, basketball, volleyball, and more. Tap any game to see details, chat with other fans, and submit scores."

---

### SCENE 4: Submitting a Live Score (60 sec)

**Screen:** Game detail → Submit button → Score form → Confirmation

**Voiceover:**
> "Here's where you become part of the action. When you're at a game, tap the submit button to report the score. Choose your submission type - a live update during the game, a period score, or the final score."

**UI Highlights:**
- "Submit Score" button
- Submission type selector (Final +10pts, Period +5pts, Live +5pts)
- Period/Quarter dropdown
- Score entry fields
- Photo bonus (+3 pts)
- Location bonus (+2 pts)

**Voiceover (continued):**
> "Enter the scores, select the period or quarter, and submit. You'll earn points for every submission - and if it's a Golden Game, you'll earn triple points! Add a photo from the game for bonus points."

**Screen:** Success confirmation with points earned

**Voiceover (continued):**
> "Your submission helps the whole community stay updated. The more you contribute, the more you earn."

---

### SCENE 5: Chat Features (45 sec)

**Screen:** Game chat → Community chat

**Voiceover:**
> "Every game has its own chat where fans can discuss the action in real-time. Cheer on your team, react to big plays, and connect with other supporters."

**UI Highlights:**
- Chat message list
- Message input
- Like button (heart)
- Reply threading
- @mention suggestions
- GIF picker

**Voiceover (continued):**
> "You can also join the general community chat to talk about Hawaii high school sports with fans across the islands. Use @mentions to tag other users, share GIFs, and like messages you enjoy."

---

### SCENE 6: Earning Raffle Entries (30 sec)

**Screen:** Leaderboard page → Raffle section

**Voiceover:**
> "Every point you earn automatically becomes a raffle entry. Check the leaderboard to see your standing and view active raffles. Prizes include gift cards, merchandise, and more - all for being an active part of the Hawaii Sports Center community."

**UI Highlights:**
- Points display
- Leaderboard rankings
- Active raffles with prizes
- Entry count

---

### SCENE 7: Closing (15 sec)

**Screen:** App overview / logo

**Voiceover:**
> "That's it! Download Hawaii Sports Center, join the community, and never miss a moment of Hawaii high school sports. See you at the game!"

---

## UI Callouts to Highlight

During editing, add visual callouts (arrows, boxes, zoom) to these elements:

1. **Login page:** Phone input field, Send Code button
2. **OTP page:** 6-digit input boxes
3. **Homepage:** LIVE badge, score display, sport filter tabs
4. **Submit form:** Points indicators (+10, +5), photo/location bonuses
5. **Chat:** Like button, reply arrow, GIF picker icon
6. **Leaderboard:** Points total, raffle entry count
