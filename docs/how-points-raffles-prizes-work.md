# How Points, Raffles & Prizes Work on Hawaii Sports Center

## Earning Points

**You earn points by participating on the app.** Here's how:

### Reporting Scores
| What you do | Points you get |
|---|---|
| Submit any score | 1 point |
| First person to report a game's score | +1 bonus point |
| Report a Golden Game (marked with a star) | Everything above x3 |

- **Max 3 points per game**
- **Max 50 points per day**

### Chatting in Game Chats
| What you do | Points you get | Daily max |
|---|---|---|
| Post a comment | 1 point | 10 per day |
| Someone likes your comment | 2 points | 20 per day |
| Someone mentions you | 1 point | 5 per day |

### Score Predictions
| How close you were | Points you get |
|---|---|
| Exact score match | 50 points |
| Top 3 closest | 25 points |
| Top 4-10 closest | 10 points |

---

## Monthly Raffles

**Every point you earn = 1 raffle entry. You don't have to do anything extra to enter -- it's automatic.**

- A new raffle runs every month
- The more points you earn, the more entries you have
- More entries = better chance of winning
- Winners are drawn randomly (weighted by entries)
- Usually 3 winners per month (1st, 2nd, 3rd place)

### Top Contributor Prize
The person with the **most points that month** gets a guaranteed bonus prize -- no luck needed, just be the most active reporter.

---

## Prizes

Prizes are created by the admin and attached to each raffle. They can be:
- **Gift cards**
- **Merchandise**
- **Cash**
- **Experiences**

Each raffle can have different prizes for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.

---

## Where Users See All This

- **Rewards page** -- shows the current raffle, your entries, prize info, top contributors leaderboard, and past winners
- **Profile page** -- shows your total points and point history

---

## Quick Summary

1. **Use the app** (report scores, chat, predict scores)
2. **Earn points automatically**
3. **Points = raffle entries** (no extra steps)
4. **Win prizes** at the end of each month

That's it! The more you participate, the better your chances.
